PRIVACY POLICY

How xxxotic plans to collect, use, and protect your information.

Draft policy only. Have a qualified attorney review and finalize before public launch. The platform's actual data practices will be confirmed and documented as features ship.

1. Information We Collect

This policy describes the categories of information xxxotic plans to collect, use, and share when you use our website and app. Specific data practices may evolve as features launch.

2. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect your name, role (dancer, club owner, or patron), and basic profile information.

3. Contact Information

We may collect your phone number and email address so you can sign in, receive notifications, and communicate within the platform.

4. Location Information

We may collect approximate or precise location to power venue search, nearby event discovery, and other location-based features. You can manage location permissions in your device settings.

5. Usage Data

We may collect data about how you interact with the Service, such as pages viewed, features used, and device information, to improve the platform.

6. Payment Information

If and when payment processing is enabled, payment data will be handled by a third-party payment processor. We do not currently store full payment card details. Additional payment-related disclosures will be added as those features go live.

7. How We Use Information

We use information to operate, secure, and improve the Service; to communicate with you; to enable bookings and venue features; to comply with legal obligations; and to protect users and the platform.

8. How We Share Information

We may share information with venues, dancers, or service providers as needed to fulfill bookings, with vendors that help us operate the platform, and where required by law.

9. Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies, similar technologies, and analytics to operate and measure the Service. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

11. User Choices

You can update or correct your information through your account. You may also request access, deletion, or other rights as described below.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

13. Children's Privacy

xxxotic is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have collected information from a child, we will delete it.

14. California / State Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, delete, or opt out of certain uses. Specific details will be added as state-by-state requirements are confirmed.

15. Updates to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy with a new effective date. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

16. Contact Information

Questions about this policy? Contact xxxotic Inc. through the channels listed on our Help & Resources page. (Mailing address to be added prior to launch.)

Effective date: To be set on launch.
