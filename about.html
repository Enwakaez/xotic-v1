ABOUT XXXOTIC

A nightlife platform built to make going out easier, smarter, and more transparent.

OUR GOAL

Going out shouldn't feel like a gamble. Patrons are tired of arriving misinformed, paying surprise prices, and walking away feeling like they didn't get what they came for. Dancers are running real businesses through scattered DMs and group chats. Club owners are juggling rosters, sections, and demand without a single source of truth.

xxxotic exists to modernize nightlife. We're building one platform where dancers can manage their schedule and grow their client base, club owners can run operations and surface demand, and patrons can plan their night — entertainment, sections, tables, bottles, and services — from their phone before they ever leave the house.

Think of it as Resy and OpenTable for restaurants, or DoorDash for delivery — but reimagined for the way nightlife actually works.
