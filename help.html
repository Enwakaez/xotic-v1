APP HELP & FAQ

How do I create an account?

Head to Sign Up, choose your role, and fill in your name, phone, and email. Club owners add their venue details too.

How do dancers add availability?

From the dancer dashboard (in the app), set the venues you're working, the nights you're available, and any blackout times. Patrons can then book inside those windows.

How do club owners list a venue?

Sign up as an owner, fill in your club details, then invite dancers to connect their schedules to your venue.

How do patrons find events?

Browse the Locations page or open the app for live events near you.

Is payment processing available yet?

Not yet — see our Payment Processing page for what's planned.

How do I download the app?

Go to the Download page. iOS and Android availability is rolling out; join the waitlist to be notified.

SAFETY & HUMAN TRAFFICKING RESOURCES

xxxotic stands against exploitation in any form. If you, or someone you know, may be in danger or being trafficked, help is available.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Warning signs of exploitation

  • Someone else holds their ID, money, or phone
  • They appear coached, scripted, or fearful when speaking
  • Signs of physical abuse, malnutrition, or sleep deprivation
  • Inability to leave a job, residence, or situation
  • Movement controlled or monitored by another person

How to seek help

  • Reach out to a trusted friend, family member, or local authority
  • Visit a hospital, library, or community center for safe contact
  • Use a public phone or borrow one to call for help if yours is monitored

National Human Trafficking Hotline

The National Human Trafficking Hotline operates 24/7 in the United States. Search "National Human Trafficking Hotline" to find the most current verified phone number, text option, and live chat resources.

Note: We're not linking a specific number directly until we verify the most current published contact details. Please confirm directly with the hotline's official site.
