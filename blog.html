FOR DANCERS

WHY DANCERS NEED BETTER BOOKING TOOLS

Most dancers are running a small business out of DMs, group chats, and screenshots. Bookings get missed. Patrons ghost. Schedules get double-booked. xxxotic gives dancers a single place to manage availability, share appearances, and turn followers into paying clients.

  • Schedule control. Set the venues, nights, and time blocks you'll actually be working.
  • Availability sharing. One booking link your fans can tap from anywhere.
  • Revenue visibility. See what you earn, where, and from which offerings.
  • Easier promotion. Push appearances directly to nearby patrons.
  • Less back-and-forth. Centralize messages so you stop losing leads in your DMs.
FOR OWNERS

WHY CLUB OWNERS NEED BETTER NIGHTLIFE OPERATIONS

If you're running a club from sticky notes and group texts, you're leaving money on the floor. xxxotic gives you live operational visibility, demand signals, and a single source of truth for who's working and who's coming through the door.

  • Operational visibility. Know who's on the schedule, who's late, and what's available right now.
  • Patron demand signals. See what's converting before peak hours hit.
  • Event promotion. Push themed nights and feature dancers directly to patrons.
  • Dancer scheduling. Coordinate the roster without a stack of group chats.
  • Better booking flow. Tables, sections, bottles, and reservations in one place.
FOR PATRONS

WHY PATRONS NEED BETTER INFORMATION BEFORE GOING OUT

Most nights out start blind. You don't know who's working, what the cover is, or what a section actually costs until you're already at the door. xxxotic gives patrons real information before they leave the house.

  • Better planning. Pick the right venue for the vibe you want.
  • Less misinformation. No more "they said it was open" surprises.
  • Clarity on events, pricing, and offerings. See what's actually available.
  • Book the night from your phone. Like Resy, OpenTable, or DoorDash — for nightlife.
